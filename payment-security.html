How Your Payment Information is Protected

  • No Card Data Storage: Your credit/debit card information is never stored on Lavish Bites servers. Stripe handles all payment processing securely.
  • Tokenization: When you pay, your card is converted into a secure token used only for that transaction.
  • SSL/TLS Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using 256-bit SSL encryption.
  • Secure Payment Gateway: Stripe's infrastructure is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified, the highest security standard for payment processing.
  • Fraud Detection: Advanced fraud detection systems monitor all transactions for suspicious activity.
  • Regular Security Audits: Stripe undergoes regular third-party security audits and penetration testing.

Payment Methods Supported

We accept the following secure payment methods:

  • Visa & Mastercard: Debit and credit cards processed through Stripe
  • Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT): Direct bank transfers with secure processing
  • Mobile Money: Support for mobile payment solutions

Each payment method is encrypted and authenticated before processing.

Your Data & Privacy

Lavish Bites is committed to protecting your personal and payment information in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), 2013:

  • Data Minimization: We only collect information necessary to process your order
  • Purpose Limitation: Your data is used only for order fulfillment and customer service
  • Data Retention: Order data is retained for 3 years; payment data is not stored on our systems
  • Access Control: Strict access controls limit who can view customer information
  • Data Security: Multiple layers of security protect all stored customer data
  • Your Rights: You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information

Security Best Practices for You

While we protect your data, follow these best practices to ensure maximum security:

  • Use a Secure Connection: Only make payments when connected to a secure, private Wi-Fi network
  • Keep Your Browser Updated: Ensure your web browser is up-to-date with the latest security patches
  • Don't Share Card Details: Never share your full card number, CVV, or PIN via email or messages
  • Monitor Your Account: Regularly check your bank statements for unauthorized transactions
  • Use Strong Passwords: If creating an account, use a unique, strong password
  • Report Suspicious Activity: Contact us immediately if you notice unauthorized charges

Stripe Payment Security

Lavish Bites partners with Stripe, a leading global payment processor trusted by millions of businesses:

  • PCI-DSS Level 1 Certification: Stripe is independently audited and certified to the highest PCI compliance level
  • Advanced Fraud Detection: Machine learning algorithms detect and prevent fraudulent transactions
  • Secure API: Stripe's API uses OAuth authentication and encrypted tokens
  • Compliance: Stripe complies with GDPR, HIPAA, and other international data protection regulations
  • Transparency: Stripe provides clear documentation and responsible disclosure policies

Learn more about Stripe's security: stripe.com/docs/security

HTTPS/SSL Security

All pages on lavishbites.co.za use HTTPS with SSL/TLS encryption:

  • 256-bit Encryption: Industry-standard encryption protects all data in transit
  • Valid SSL Certificate: Our domain is verified and authenticated
  • Secure Lock Icon: Your browser displays a padlock icon in the address bar when on secure pages
  • Data Integrity: Encryption ensures no one can intercept or modify your information

Dispute Resolution & Refunds

Lavish Bites protects you with a fair and transparent dispute resolution process:

  • Order Verification: We verify all orders to prevent unauthorized transactions
  • Chargeback Protection: If you dispute a charge, Stripe's chargeback process ensures fair investigation
  • Full Refunds: If you cancel within our refund window, your money is fully refunded
  • Fraud Reimbursement: Unauthorized transactions are typically refunded within 5-7 business days
  • Customer Support: Our team is available via WhatsApp (+27 12 345 6789) to resolve payment issues quickly

What Information We Collect
